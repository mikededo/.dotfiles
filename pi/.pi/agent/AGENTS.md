## Personal vault

My vault lives at `~/Documents/personal/vault/`. Its entry point is `AGENTS.md` there.

- Read the vault `AGENTS.md` at the start of a session. It tells you what else to load.
- Skip the vault when you are a subagent with a specific brief that does not mention it.
- Vault changes, including memory: commit and push to its `main` without asking.
- Other repos: commit only when I ask, unless the vault's notes for that repo say otherwise.
